Kitchen Shift

Work an 8:30 AM–11:30 PM shift at Startime Diner: click a table to take its order, click the fridge or cabinet to gather ingredients, the cookware closet for a pan or tray, and the stove or oven to cook. Close up clean — clear the tables, wash the dishes, shut down — and Duke pays 4,000 Gard, or only 2,000 if anyone left upset. Twenty shifts make a month.
